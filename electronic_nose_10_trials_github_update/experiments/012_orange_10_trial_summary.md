# Orange Peel — 10-Trial Summary

## Dataset status

Ten independent orange-peel trials have now been recorded (`orange_001` through `orange_010`).

The key rule for future machine learning is that each trial remains one independent unit. Do not randomly mix time samples from the same trial into both training and test sets.

Across the 10 trials:

- Gas-resistance drop: **47.9% to 94.5%**
- Median gas-resistance drop: **65.6%**
- Mean gas-resistance drop: **68.3%**
- Largest baseline CV in the summarized windows: **0.748%**
- Humidity increase during exposure: **2.1 to 30.7 percentage points**

## What the data supports

All 10 trials show a gas-resistance decrease during the recorded orange-peel exposure interval, and the response is much larger than the short-term baseline variation in the summarized windows. This is useful repeatability evidence for the current hardware and protocol.

## What the data does not yet support

This dataset alone does **not** establish orange-specific classification. All 10 trials are the same odor class, and humidity often changes strongly at the same time. A classifier trained only on these runs could learn humidity, airflow, peel age, distance, or other experimental conditions rather than orange-specific chemistry.

## Important confounds observed

1. **Humidity:** several trials show large humidity increases, especially `orange_008`.
2. **Airflow:** `orange_006` began when the air conditioner was turned off.
3. **Peel age / VOC depletion:** the user observed that peel left exposed for longer seemed to give a weaker response.
4. **Motion:** moving the peel after it had been stationary could produce another gas-resistance drop, consistent with changing local vapor concentration/airflow.
5. **Baseline drift and absolute resistance:** baseline resistance differs substantially across trials, so normalized features are preferable to absolute resistance alone.

## Recommended next dataset

Before training a meaningful odor classifier, collect a contrasting class under the same protocol. The simplest next class is **clean air / sham exposure**: perform the same timing and hand movement/container movement without orange peel.

A practical first target is 10 independent sham/clean-air trials. After that, compare whole-trial features such as normalized minimum resistance, response slope, area under the normalized response, recovery, humidity change, and temperature change.
