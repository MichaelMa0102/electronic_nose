# Orange Peel Trial 001

## Purpose

First repeatable-response test of the BME688 using orange peel.

## Hardware

- ESP32-S3-N16R8
- BME688 breakout
- I2C address: `0x77`
- SDA: GPIO 8
- SCL: GPIO 9
- Supply: 3.3 V

## Sensor configuration

- Temperature oversampling: 8x
- Humidity oversampling: 2x
- Pressure oversampling: 4x
- IIR filter: size 3
- Gas heater: 320 C for 150 ms
- Serial baud: 115200

## Trial timing

The exposure interval was recorded manually during the experiment:

- Baseline: `< 70000 ms`
- Orange peel exposure: `70000–140000 ms`
- Recovery: `> 140000 ms`

The orange peel was placed near the sensor during the exposure interval.

## Files

- Raw sensor data: `data/raw/orange_001.csv`
- Labeled data: `data/processed/orange_001_labeled.csv`
- Firmware used: `firmware/bme688_logger/bme688_logger.ino`

## Quick observations

- Samples recorded: 358
- Baseline mean gas resistance: 397.7 kOhm
- Exposure mean gas resistance: 250.9 kOhm
- Minimum gas resistance during exposure: 197.0 kOhm at 115365 ms
- Late-recovery mean gas resistance (>=350 s): 387.8 kOhm
- Relative drop from baseline mean to exposure minimum: 50.5 %

## Notes

This trial shows a strong, reversible response, but one trial is not enough to claim odor classification.
Repeat independent trials under the same geometry and timing before training a model.
